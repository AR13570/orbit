# Gravity mode

Implement the concept approved in chat: a mode chooser for Zen and Gravity, an endless seeded sequence of orbital puzzles, repeated drag-release launches while earlier projectiles fly, distinct muted projectile colors with time-fading tails, optional mutual projectile gravity, unlimited retries and skipping. Preserve the existing Zen engine, generator and behavior.

Gravity uses a fixed square field fitted inside safe HUD margins on desktop and mobile. A stable fixed-step gravitational simulation supports sustained orbits without checkpoint assistance. Track signed angular travel around attractive bodies; only living projectiles with a completed loop count, without a radius cutoff. Separate-body goals recognize the most recent local capture and exclude shared loops. Goals include a single loop, simultaneous orbiters, opposite directions, and separate populated bodies. Generate seeded randomized objectives and constrained layouts from the first level, vary positions/radii and zero to two repellent obstacles that measurably delay a verified solution, and validate a witness sequence using the same simulation and goal rules. Reject candidates that fail. Avoid consecutive identical families. Use bounded candidate retries without a preset level catalog or fixed introductory sequence. Gravity velocity is scaled by 1.6 and forces by 2.56 to retain familiar orbit shapes at a faster pace. Keep generated witness shots invisible.

Level completion briefly celebrates then advances exactly once. Retry keeps the layout and seed; skip advances without claiming a completion. Bound active projectiles and tail memory. Pause freezes simulation and feedback, cancels held input, traps menu focus, and requires manual resume after hiding the page. Interaction changes revalidate the current seeded level and reset shots. Keep renderer, engine and generator modules separate. No new dependencies, commits or pushes. Leave the shared browser and server untouched.
